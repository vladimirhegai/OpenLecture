# ELEC 221: Course map

## Electric Circuits

> **Info:** This map summarizes syllabus scope. Upload lectures and tutorials for detailed source-grounded notes and actual tutorial problems.

- Circuit elements, Ohm's law, KCL and KVL
- Dividers, nodal and mesh analysis, Thevenin and Norton
- RC and RL initial-condition and step responses
- RLC models, initial conditions and step responses
- Sinusoidal response, phasors and AC power

Study notes should include explanations, justified equations, useful diagrams, checked worked examples, original practice, and common mistakes. Course tools and limitations are listed in the study guidance.
