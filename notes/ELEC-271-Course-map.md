## October 29 midterm · study these three sections

**Thursday October 29, during class · 40 minutes · Weeks 1–5 only.** Week 6 after reading week is excluded. The instructor’s announcement controls the scope below.

1. [01 · Binary Numbers — Midterm](http://127.0.0.1:8000/courses/d0b18428-9c03-4bbf-87af-7a1169d667ab/docs/a20960cb-7622-43c0-8daf-c706abd4b2e5)
2. [02 · Introduction to Logic Circuits — Midterm](http://127.0.0.1:8000/courses/d0b18428-9c03-4bbf-87af-7a1169d667ab/docs/120cfcb1-7126-46ec-9e94-3780eabbd8cd)
3. [03 · Implementation Technology — Midterm (Week 5)](http://127.0.0.1:8000/courses/d0b18428-9c03-4bbf-87af-7a1169d667ab/docs/22833a28-26bd-49d0-abf5-658b3a0fe16b)

| Main note | What to study |
|---|---|
| Section 1 | Binary ↔ decimal ↔ hexadecimal, including fractions and limited precision. |
| Section 2 | Description ↔ truth table ↔ function ↔ gates; complements; Venn and timing diagrams; canonical SOP/POS; NAND–NAND and NOR–NOR conversions. |
| Section 3 — Week 5 | NMOS/PMOS switches, NMOS and CMOS gates, transistor counts, drawing CMOS from a function, and recovering a function from CMOS. |

**PS2: study Q1 and Q3–Q5. Skip Q2 and Q6–Q8 for this midterm.** These exclusions do not remove general Boolean-algebra or CMOS skills from the announced topic list.

After the three sections, attempt the supplied previous-year midterm with a 40-minute limit. Use its solutions to check reasoning and notation; use this announcement to decide what is examinable this year.
