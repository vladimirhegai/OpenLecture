# ELEC 226/326: Course map

## Probability & Random Processes

> **Info:** This map summarizes syllabus scope. Upload lectures and tutorials for detailed source-grounded notes and actual tutorial problems.

- Week 1: Probability models, sets, sample spaces and events
- Week 2: Axioms, sampling, conditional probability and Bayes
- Week 3: Random variables, discrete PMFs and CDFs
- Week 4: Continuous distributions, densities and CDFs
- Week 5: Expectation, variance, moments and bounds
- Week 6: Functions of random variables, moments and information
- Week 7: Transformations, characteristic functions and entropy
- Week 8: Multiple random variables and decision-making
- Week 9: Pairs, marginals, conditional distributions and independence
- Week 10: One function of two random variables
- Week 11: Two functions, Jacobians and Gaussian random vectors
- Week 12: Limit theorems and basic stochastic processes

Study notes should include explanations, justified equations, useful diagrams, checked worked examples, original practice, and common mistakes. Course tools and limitations are listed in the study guidance.
