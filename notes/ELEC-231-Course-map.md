# ELEC 231: Course map

## Mathematical Methods I for Electrical and Computer Engineering

> **Info:** This map summarizes syllabus scope. Upload lectures and tutorials for detailed source-grounded notes and actual tutorial problems.

- Week 1: Modelling and complex numbers
- Week 2: ODE classification and initial value problems
- Week 3: Separable and linear ODEs; existence and uniqueness
- Week 4: Exact equations, integrating factors and second-order equations
- Week 5: Constant-coefficient second-order equations and RLC models
- Week 6: Reduction of order and undetermined coefficients
- Week 7: Laplace transforms and the Heaviside step function
- Week 8: Laplace initial-value problems, discontinuities and impulses
- Week 9: Convolution and first-order systems
- Week 10: Linear systems and real/complex eigenvalues
- Week 11: Repeated eigenvalues, variation of parameters and phase planes
- Week 12: Euler, Runge-Kutta and error analysis

Study notes should include explanations, justified equations, useful diagrams, checked worked examples, original practice, and common mistakes. Course tools and limitations are listed in the study guidance.
