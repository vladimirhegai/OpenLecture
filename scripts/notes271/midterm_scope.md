# ELEC 271 midterm announcement

Provided by the student from the instructor's announcement.

The midterm takes place on Thursday October 29 during class time and is 40 minutes long.
Only content covered in Weeks 1–5 is tested. Week 6 after the reading week break is excluded.

## Section 1: Binary Numbers

- Convert between binary, decimal, and hexadecimal, including fractional values.

## Section 2: Introduction to Logic Circuits

- Create a truth table from a description.
- Complement a function.
- Venn diagrams and timing diagrams.
- Canonical functions from truth tables; SOP and POS forms.
- Draw logic gates for canonical functions or functions reduced using Boolean algebra.
- Work backward from timing diagrams or gate drawings to the implemented function.
- Convert SOP to NAND–NAND and POS to NOR–NOR networks.

## Section 3: Implementation Technology — Week 5 content only

- Transistor switches.
- NMOS and CMOS logic gates.
- Draw a CMOS circuit to implement a function.
- Interpret a CMOS drawing to find its function.
